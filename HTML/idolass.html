Description

Background

Htoo Eain Thin was born on 1 July 1963 in Pathein, Ayeyarwady Region, Myanmar. He attended Basic Education High School No. 3 Pathein and later studied at Pathein Regional College. In 1981, his family moved to Mawlamyine, where he continued his education and earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Physics.

Musical Career

Htoo Eain Thin began his music career with his debut album Naryi Baw Mha Myet-Yay Zet Mya (Tear Drops on the Clock) in 1986. At a time when Burmese pop music was dominated by Western cover songs, he became known for creating original music and lyrics. His style blended Western influences such as jazz, rock, and pop with emotional Burmese melodies and lyrics, making his songs unique and widely loved.

Success and Legacy

Throughout his 18-year career, Htoo Eain Thin released 14 solo albums and produced many memorable songs that remain popular today. Albums such as Atta Bon Saung Khe Mya and Akyinna Einmet brought him great success, while songs like Mei-Lai-Taw, Min-Ma-Shi-De-Nauk, and Hsway-De became Burmese classics.
